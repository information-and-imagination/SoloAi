# SoloAi™

**SoloAi™** is a work-in-progress experiment exploring how independently an AI agent can develop, launch, and operate a small online business.

The idea is simple:

**Give an AI agent a goal, a limited starting budget, defined operating boundaries, and the tools to work — then see what it can accomplish.**

Rather than directing every business decision, SoloAi will explore how much of the process can be handled by the agent itself, including:

* Identifying and evaluating opportunities
* Developing business ideas
* Planning and documenting decisions
* Creating products or content
* Establishing an online presence
* Managing a limited operating budget
* Measuring results and adapting its strategy

Initial experiments are expected to begin with very small amounts of capital, approximately **$50 per agent**, with human approval retained for financial, legal, contractual, and other consequential actions.

SoloAi will also serve as a real-world test project for the **Cyples Engineering Methodology (CEM)**, with agents maintaining project documentation so their goals, decisions, progress, and changes can be reviewed.

## Status

**Work in Progress**

SoloAi is currently in the concept and early-development stage. The architecture, operating rules, experiments, and business models will evolve as the project develops.

The experiment isn't intended to prove that AI can simply generate content.

The more interesting question is:

**Can an AI recognize an opportunity, make reasonable business decisions, work within limited resources, adapt to results, and build something sustainable?**
